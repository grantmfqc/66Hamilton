Licence to Occupy for Serviced Accommodation

THIS AGREEMENT is made on the date of final electronic execution by both parties.

Parties:

  1. GRANT DENNIS (the "Licensor")
  2. _____, identified as the Primary Licensee in Section 1 of the attached Onboarding Portal (the "Licensee")

Background:

A. The Licensor is the master lease holder of the residential property located at Apartment 1, 66 Hamilton Road, Herne Bay, Auckland 1011, New Zealand (the "Premises"), and possesses all necessary commercial and legal authority to grant non-exclusive temporary licenses to occupy the property.

B. The Licensor operates the property as premium, short-term commercial serviced accommodation.

C. The Licensee wishes to occupy the property for a temporary, transient period of exactly _____, subject to the active delivery of commercial services by the Licensor.

D. The parties explicitly agree that this arrangement is not a residential tenancy and is expressly excluded from the operations of the New Zealand Residential Tenancies Act 1986 pursuant to Section 5 of that Act (relating to temporary, transient, and serviced accommodation).

Operative Terms:

1. Grant of Licence

1.1 The Licensor grants to the Licensee a personal, revocable, and non-exclusive Licence to occupy the Premises for a fixed period of exactly _____, alongside the right to use the chattels, furniture, and fittings inside the Premises.

1.2 This agreement does not create any estate or legal interest in the land, nor does it grant exclusive possession of the Premises to the Licensee. The legal rights remain under the control of the Licensor.

2. Term & Definite Expiry

2.1 The term of this Licence shall be for exactly _____, beginning on the Commencement Date and automatically terminating without notice on the Expiry Date as defined in the execution schedule.

2.2 The Licensee acknowledges that there is no right of renewal, no right of rollover, and no transition into a periodic residential arrangement upon the passing of the Expiry Date.

3. Booking Securement, Payment Terms & Cancellation

3.1 Property Availability Status: The Premises shall remain actively advertised, listed on the open market, and available for booking to third parties until the upfront payment terms have been fully cleared.

4. Bundled Services & Mandatory Right of Entry

4.1 The weekly Licence Fee includes the active provision of the following commercial services by the Licensor:

  • Full provisioning and baseline usage allocation of residential utilities (electricity, gas, water, and unlimited high-speed broadband internet).
  • A mandatory, professional housekeeping and interior cleaning service conducted once per calendar week.

4.2 Erosion of Quiet Enjoyment: Because this is a commercial serviced accommodation contract, the Licensor, their property managers, and contracted housekeeping staff retain an absolute, non-negotiable, and unhindered weekly right of entry to perform the mandatory cleaning services, verify chattel safety, and inspect the status of the asset. The Licensee cannot refuse, delay, or restrict this entry.

5. 24-Hour Inventory Reconciliation Window

5.1 A complete inventory log of high-end furniture, electronics, and household chattels will be provided to the Licensee upon arrival.

5.2 The Licensee has exactly 24 hours from physical check-in to inspect the Premises and notify the Licensor via email or WhatsApp of any missing inventory or pre-existing damage.

5.3 If no discrepancy notice is received within this 24-hour window, the Premises and all listed chattels shall be legally deemed to have been delivered in immaculate condition. The Licensee shall be held fully liable for any defects, damages, or missing items recorded at the final checkout inspection.

6. Winter Utility Fair Use Cap Clause

6.1 The bundled weekly Licence Fee covers standard domestic utility usage up to a maximum baseline actual cost of NZD $600.00 (inclusive of GST) per calendar month.

6.2 In the event that actual utility statements (comprising cumulative electricity, gas, water, and internet bills) exceed $600.00 NZD in any single calendar month of the term, the Licensee shall be directly liable for the excess amount.

6.3 The Licensor will provide copy statements of the utility bills to the Licensee. The excess amount, plus a 15% administrative and processing surcharge, will be invoiced immediately to the Licensee and must be paid via electronic transfer within five (5) business days of the invoice date. Failure to pay constitutes a material breach of this Licence.

7. Optional Linen & Bed Sheet Service

7.1 The mandatory weekly cleaning service does not include bed sheet laundering and linen replacement.

7.2 If requested in writing by the Licensee, the Licensor can provide a complete premium linen and bed sheet replacement service at an additional cost of NZD $45.00 + GST ($51.75 incl. GST) per bed, per week. This service will be invoiced separately and must be paid in advance alongside the main Licence Fee block.

8. Conduct, House Rules & Liability Penalties

8.1 Occupancy Restrictions: Only the specific individuals declared in Section 2 of the attached Onboarding Portal are permitted to reside in the Premises. Sub-licensing rooms or hosting additional permanent overnight guests is strictly prohibited.

8.2 Quiet Hours & Social Gatherings: The Premises is located in a strict residential building. Quiet hours must be observed between 10:00 PM and 7:00 AM daily. No parties or large social gatherings are permitted. If building management or noise control protocols require a security guard call-out due to loud behavior from the occupants, a mandatory NZD $250.00 enforcement fee will be billed instantly to the Licensee, alongside serving as immediate grounds for Licence revocation.

8.3 Fire Alarm False Call-Outs: Smoking and vaping are strictly prohibited inside the Premises or on the balconies. If the occupants trigger a false fire alarm via cooking negligence, smoking, or tampering with sensors that results in a Fire and Emergency New Zealand (FENZ) call-out, the Licensee is strictly liable for the full penalty fine levied by emergency services (historically $1,000.00+ GST), plus a 15% administrative fee.

8.4 Supervision of Minors: In strict accordance with New Zealand child safety laws, no children under the age of 14 years may be left unattended or without adult supervision anywhere within the Premises or common property of the building complex at any time.

9. Option to Request Extension

9.1 The Licensee may request an extension to the term of this Licence (for a block period of between 1 and 90 additional days) by providing written notice to the Licensor no later than thirty (30) days prior to the Expiry Date.

9.2 The Licensor retains absolute discretion to accept or deny an extension request based on apartment availability, master lease parameters, or past conduct.

9.3 If an extension is mutually agreed, it will only become legally binding upon the execution of a formal "Licence Extension Addendum" and the receipt of 100% of the extension fee block in cleared funds at least ten (10) days prior to the original Expiry Date. All commercial services and weekly cleaning entry mandates will continue throughout any extension.

10. Immediate Revocation & Eviction

10.1 If the Licensee commits a material breach of this agreement, fails to clear utility overage invoices, or violates structural building rules, the Licensor reserves the right to revoke this Licence immediately upon written notice.

10.2 Upon revocation, the Licensee's personal permission to occupy the space terminates instantly. They must vacate the premises and remove all personal belongings within twenty-four (24) hours without recourse to the New Zealand Tenancy Tribunal.

11. Jurisdiction

11.1 This agreement is governed exclusively by the laws of New Zealand. Any contractual disputes that cannot be settled by mutual agreement shall be directed to the courts of New Zealand.
